Abstract

Despite rapid progress, instruction-guided text-to-speech (TTS) systems still rely on paired expressive speech-instruction datasets for emotional speech generation. To alleviate this burden, we introduce a cross-lingual instruction distillation (CLID) method, which transfers the instruction-following capability of a pretrained TTS model across different instruction languages.

Using semantically aligned English-Korean instructions with the same spoken text, CLID distills the teacher’s speech-token distributions into a student. We further apply CFG to construct guided teacher targets and distill them into a Korean-SFT student, yielding CLID-TTS with improved emotion controllability and speech quality.

Experimental results demonstrate that CLID improves Korean instruction following without paired Korean instruction-speech data, while CLID-TTS further improves emotion classification accuracy and speech intelligibility.

02 / Method

From aligned instructions
to expressive speech.

CLID transfers instruction-following behavior across languages. CFG-guided distillation further shapes the teacher targets used to train CLID-TTS.

  1. 01

    Bilingual instruction pairs

    Construct semantically aligned Korean and English instructions from Korean emotional speech. The spoken text is shared across the instruction languages.

  2. 02

    Cross-lingual instruction distillation

    Align the teacher’s speech-token distributions under English instructions with the student’s distributions under Korean instructions.

  3. 03

    CFG-guided teacher targets

    Apply classifier-free guidance to construct teacher targets and distill them into a Korean-SFT student, producing CLID-TTS.
